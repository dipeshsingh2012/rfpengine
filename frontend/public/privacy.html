Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

Summary: RFPEngine and the RFPEngine Chrome Extension process questionnaire content solely to generate answers from your enterprise knowledge base. We never sell your data, use your confidential data to train public foundation models, or track your browsing activity.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to RFPEngine (the "Service"), including the web platform hosted at https://www.rfpengine.net and the RFPEngine Response Assistant browser extension. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting sensitive enterprise data.

2. Data We Collect and Process

We only collect and process data necessary to provide and operate the RFP automation services:

  • Questionnaire Content: When you click "Scan Form" in the browser extension or upload questionnaire files to the web application, question text is parsed to generate relevant answers from your knowledge base.
  • Knowledge Base Documents: Files and passages you upload (e.g. security whitepapers, SOC 2 reports, compliance documents) are indexed in your tenant's isolated vector and relational databases.
  • Account & Preferences: User settings, assigned reviewer roles, tenant IDs, and approval statuses saved during questionnaire drafting workflows.

3. What We DO NOT Collect

  • We do not track your general web browsing history or website activity outside of the questionnaires you explicitly scan.
  • We do not access passwords, cookies, financial information, payment data, or personal communications.
  • We do not run continuous background scraping scripts; form scanning is executed only upon manual user initiation.

4. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is strictly utilized for the following functions:

  • Executing hybrid search (BM25 keyword search + dense vector retrieval) against your uploaded documents.
  • Synthesizing grounded answers using enterprise LLM inference (Google Cloud Vertex AI).
  • Populating approved answers back into your active web forms upon your explicit review and confirmation.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256. Infrastructure is hosted on Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run, Vertex AI) and secure SOC 2 certified managed database providers (Neon PostgreSQL, Elastic Cloud, Pinecone Serverless).

6. Chrome Web Store Compliance Certification

In adherence to the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies:

  • User data is never sold to data brokers or third parties.
  • User data is not used or transferred for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose (RFP questionnaire assistance).
  • User data is not used or transferred to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You maintain complete ownership and control of your knowledge base. You can delete indexed documents, passages, and questionnaire workspaces at any time through the RFPEngine Knowledge Base dashboard, which purges them synchronously across PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch, and Pinecone.
